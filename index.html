MeetingTax

Meetings are the largest invisible line item in any company. Eight people in a room for an hour is $700 of payroll, and it shows up nowhere. MeetingTax puts the meter on the wall.

Start the meter

Add who's in the room by role and rate, start the clock when the meeting starts, and watch the cost tick live - per person, blended hourly rate, and what this meeting costs per year if it runs weekly. When it ends, you get the verdict: cheap enough, normal, pricey, or should have been an email. Everything stays in your browser.
